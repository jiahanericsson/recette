---
title: almond brownie (vincent pilon)
ingredients:
- name: Butter
  quantity: 188
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 188
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 188
  unit: g
- name: dark chocolate 64%
  quantity: 188
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 30
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 2
  unit: g
- name: roasted granulated almonds
  quantity: 97
  unit: g
steps:
- melt chocolate
- cream butter and sugar with paddle. Add eggs, salt, melted chocolate, flour, and almonds in this order
- pipe in half sphere flexi molds (fill to top)
- place 2 silpains on top and bake  at 340°F for 18 - 20  minutes
- freeze until ready to use or consume
