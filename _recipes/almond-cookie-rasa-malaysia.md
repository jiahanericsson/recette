---
title: almond cookie (rasa malaysia)
ingredients:
- name: Butter
  quantity: 130
  unit: g
- name: powdered sugar
  quantity: 70
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 2
  unit: g
- name: corn flour
  quantity: 80
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 120
  unit: g
- name: sliced almonds
  quantity: 60
  unit: g
steps:
- lightly toast almonds
- cream butter, sugar, and salt until smooth
- incorporate sifted corn flour and flour
- gently mix in sliced almonds
- roll out dough until 1 cm thick and cut out desired shapes with cookie cutter
- freeze to keep or bake at 325 F for 20 minutes
- (optionally add 5 g of tea powder or other powders for flavoring)
- '[makes ~ 50 small cookies]'
---
