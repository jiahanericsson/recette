---
title: biscuits a la cuillere (ladyfingers)
ingredients:
- name: Yolk
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: sugar (1)
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: egg white
  quantity: 75
  unit: g
- name: sugar (2)
  quantity: 12
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 62
  unit: g
steps:
- Cre am the yolks and sugar (1)
- Beat the egg whites with sugar (2)
- Very quickly with a whisk mix the yolk mixture and  the beaten egg whites
- Delicately fold in the flour
- Pipe in spirals with pastry bag and pastry tip 10 on baking paper
- Bake at 190 C
---
