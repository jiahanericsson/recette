---
title: Caramel Fleur de Sel
ingredients:
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: Vanilla Pod
  quantity: 2
  unit: pc
- name: cream
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: fleur de sel
  quantity: 6
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 280
  unit: g
steps:
- Heat the cream
- Heat a little of the sugar and scraped vanilla pod in a saucepan until it caramelizes
- Continue adding sugar and cook a dry caramel
- Turn down the heat and slowly cook to desired color
- Add in the warm cream, little by little
- Keep stirring until the caramel un-seizes and the mixture isn’t cloudy anymore
- Cook until a soft caramel, remove from heat and add in the salt
- Transfer to a small heatproof safe bowl and leave to cool till about 40 C
- Blend in the soft butter using an immersion blender without incorporating any air in
- Store it in bottles and refrigerate till it is hard
---
