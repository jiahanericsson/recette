---
title: carrot cake
ingredients:
- name: Flour
  quantity: 240
  unit: g
- name: baking soda
  quantity: 9
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 4
  unit: g
- name: cinnamon
  quantity: 5
  unit: g
- name: nutmeg
  quantity: 1
  unit: g
- name: ginger
  quantity: 2
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: oil
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: grated carrots
  quantity: 450
  unit: g
- name: walnuts (optional)
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
steps:
- whisk sugar and oil
- add in eggs one by one
- sift in flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, and ginger
- incorporate carrots and walnuts, if using
- divide into cupcakes or layer cakes, fill up 3/4 full
- freeze to keep
- bake at 350 F for 14 to 18 minutes for cupcakes or 40 minutes for layer cakes (do the toothpick test)
---
