---
title: cheesecake (Eva Ericsson)
ingredients:
- name: cream cheese (3 8oz packages)
  quantity: 675
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 5
  unit: pc
- name: sugar (1)
  quantity: 160
  unit: g
- name: sour cream (1 pint)
  quantity: 465
  unit: g
- name: sugar (2)
  quantity: 105
  unit: g
- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 2
  unit: tsp
steps:
- mix sugar (1), cream cheese, eggs, and vanilla extract.  beat until smooth
- bake at 375 F for 25 minutes
- take out of oven, let stand for 15 minutes
- mix sour cream and sugar (2) together, pour over the top of the cake carefully
- bake at 475 F for 20 minutes
- take out of oven and cool at room temperature
- keep in fridge
---
