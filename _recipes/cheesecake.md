---
title: cheesecake
ingredients:
- name: philadelphia cream cheese
  quantity: 410
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 120
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 15
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 92
  unit: g
- name: Yolk
  quantity: 15
  unit: g
- name: cream
  quantity: 30
  unit: g
steps:
- mix cream cheese, sugar, and flour
- add the eggs and yolks gradually
- finish by adding the cream
- fill molds
- bake at 100 C for 35 minutes (depending on size)
- freeze before unmolding
- makes ~20 tartelette pcs
