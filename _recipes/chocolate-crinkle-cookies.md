---
title: Chocolate Crinkle Cookies
ingredients:
- name: unsweetened cocoa powder
  quantity: 120
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: oil (canola or grapeseed)
  quantity: 110
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 10
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 4
  unit: g
- name: baking powder
  quantity: 10
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 240
  unit: g
- name: powdered sugar
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
steps:
- mix cocoa, sugar, and oil
- slowly incorporate eggs and vanilla extract
- incorporate salt, baking powder, and flour sifted together
- cover and chill for 4 hours
- roll into 1 inch balls and freeze to keep
- when ready to bake, roll balls into powdered sugar
- bake at 350 F for 10 minutes
- '[makes ~ 6 dozen]'
---
