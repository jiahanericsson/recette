---
title: chocolate madeleine (vincent pilon)
ingredients:
- name: Butter
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 150
  unit: g
- name: baking powder
  quantity: 4
  unit: g
- name: cocoa powder
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
steps:
- Combine the softened butter and the sugar
- Add  eggs, then flour sifted with baking powder and cocoa powder
- Rest the batter a minimum of 2 hours
- Bake at 390°F for approximately 12mn (vent open)
---
