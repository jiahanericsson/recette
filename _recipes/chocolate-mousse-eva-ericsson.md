---
title: chocolate mousse (Eva Ericsson)
ingredients:
- name: Dark Chocolate
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: powdered sugar
  quantity: 60
  unit: g
- name: strong coffee (3/4 cup)
  quantity: 60
  unit: g
- name: egg yolk
  quantity: 4
  unit: pc
- name: whipping cream
  quantity: 1160
  unit: g
steps:
- melt chocolate over a bain marie
- mix sugar, coffee, and egg yolks together until smooth
- pour mixture into the chocolate and stir vigorously until the mixture is smooth
- take the bowl off the bain marie.
- whip cream lightly and mix it with the chocolate mixture
- pour into small cups and keep in fridge
- '[makes ~30 servings]'
---
