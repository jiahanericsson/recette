  quantity: 2

- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 4
  unit: g
- name: bittersweet chocolate
  quantity: 142
  unit: g
steps:
- sift flour, cocoa, and baking soda together
- beat butter and both sugars together on medium with paddle attachment until soft, creamy, and homogenous, about 3 minutes
- beat in salt and vanilla
- add all dry ingredients at once and mix until dough forms big, moist curds
- toss in chocolate pieces and mix to incorporate
- roll out until 1/2 inch thick, cut, refrigerate or freeze
- bake for 12 minutes without opening the oven at 325 F
- makes about 36 1.5 inches cut cookies
