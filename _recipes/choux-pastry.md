---
title: choux pastry
ingredients:
- name: milk
  quantity: 350
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 350
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 14
  unit: g
- name: sugar
  quantity: 28
  unit: g
- name: butter
  quantity: 280
  unit: g
- name: flour
  quantity: 450
  unit: g
- name: eggs
  quantity: 700
  unit: g
steps:
- bring water, milk, sat, sugar, and butter to a boil
- add sifted flour and stir vigorously until fully absorbed
- immediately dump out mass into kitchenaid with whisk attachment
- beat on low and slowly incorporate all the eggs at room temperature
- pipe out 3-3.5 cm puffs (6g) using a 10 mm tip
- freeze if not immediately using.
- when baking, place frozen choux in 260 C convection oven and turn off the oven.
- after choux rises with vent closed, turn the oven back on to 170 C without opening it and bake until done
- if baking with hearth oven, bake at 180 C
---
