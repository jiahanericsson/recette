---
title: cookies - blondie
ingredients:
- name: Flour
  quantity: 275
  unit: g
- name: baking soda
  quantity: 3
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 3
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 165
  unit: g
- name: brown sugar
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 60
  unit: g
- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 15
  unit: g
- name: egg
  quantity: 1
  unit: pc
- name: Yolk
  quantity: 1
  unit: pc
- name: jivara chocolate chunks
  quantity: 90
  unit: g
- name: dulcey chocolate chunks
  quantity: 90
  unit: g
- name: manjari chocolate chunks
  quantity: 90
  unit: g
- name: toasted hazelnut pieces
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: praline crumbs for rolling (optional)
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
steps:
- sift flour, baking soda, and salt; set aside
- cream together butter, brown sugar, and sugar
- add vanilla extract, egg, and yolk to mixture and mix well
- add sifted dry ingredients and mix until dough just comes together
- gently mix in chocolate chunks and nuts
- roll into 30 - 45g balls to bake or store
- 'optional: roll cookie dough in praline crumbs before baking'
- bake at 325 F for 12 - 15 min depending on size
