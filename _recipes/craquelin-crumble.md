---
title: craquelin (crumble)
ingredients:
- name: butter
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: cassonade or brown sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: regular or cake flour
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: coloring powder or berry powder (optional)
  quantity: 0.5
  unit: g
steps:
- combine all the ingredients
- spread between two sheets, roll out, and freeze until use.
