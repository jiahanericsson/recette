---
title: creme d’amande (almond cream)
ingredients:
- name: Butter
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: almond powder
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: flour (optional)
  quantity: 25
  unit: g
- name: rum
  quantity: 20
  unit: g
- name: vanilla exract
  quantity: 2
  unit: g
steps:
- cream sugar w/ softened butter
- add almond powder and mix well
- add eggs one by one
- beat the mixture to blanch it and get volumn
- add rum and vanilla extract
- pour in sifted flour
---
