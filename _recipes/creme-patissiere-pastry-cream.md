---
title: creme patissiere (pastry cream)
ingredients:
- name: Milk
  quantity: 1000
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: yolks
  quantity: 60
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: Custard Powder
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: Vanilla Pod
  quantity: 1
  unit: pc
- name: Butter
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
steps:
- Boil the milk with the scraped vanilla bean
- Mix the sugar, eggs and yolks together, well
- Add the custard powder and flour, sifted together
- Add a little hot milk to this mixture and pour all back into the casserole
- Stir continuously, let boil for 3 minutes
- Add the butter and mix
- Remove from casserole and cover with plastic film in contact with the cream
- Cool immediately
---
