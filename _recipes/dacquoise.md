---
title: dacquoise
ingredients:
- name: egg white
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 115
  unit: g
- name: almond powder (coarse)
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
steps:
- Shift the almond powder and the icing sugar together
- Whisk the egg white with the sugar added in 3 times
- Fold in the almond powder mixture with a flexible s patula
- Pipe with a pastry tip size 8 or 10, in the form of  a tart
- Bake at 180 C
- Remove from oven when it is moist, lightly colored and firm
---
