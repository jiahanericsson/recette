---
title: feuilletage inversé (laminated dough)
ingredients:
- name: butter (1)
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: flour (1)
  quantity: 130
  unit: g
- name: flour (2)
  quantity: 270
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 10
  unit: g
- name: water (approx)
  quantity: 140
  unit: g
steps:
- Make a firm détrempe with the flour (2), salt and water
- Leave to rest for half hour
- Mix butter (1) with flour (1)
- Enclose the détrempe in the butter
- Give 5 tours using either simple turns or double tu rns,  following you regular routine
- (for chocolate feuilletage, substitute flour (1) w/ 100g flour and 80g cocoa powder)
- (for pistachio feuilletage, add 75g of pistachio paste to the butter mixture)
---
