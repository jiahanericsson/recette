---
title: feuilletage (laminated dough)
ingredients:
- name: Flour
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: butter (1)
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 8
  unit: g
- name: water (approx)
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: butter (2)
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
steps:
- Rub the flour with the butter (1)
- Make a well
- Rub the flour with the butter (1)
- Make a well
- Dissolve salt in water and add water to the center  of the well
- Mix the flour and water gradually
- Knead without giving body to the dough
- Make the dough into a ball and cut an “X” into the  top with a knife
- Let the dough rest for 30 minutes in the fridge. Th is dough is called the “détrempe”
- Soften butter (2) and form it in
- Enclose butter (2) in the détrempe. (This is now ca lled the “pâton”)
- Give either two simple turns (=2 folds) or two doub le turns (=3 folds) to the pâton
- Let the pâton rest for a minimum of 30 minutes in th e refrigerator
- Give two more simple turns
- Let the pâton rest for a minimum of 30 minutes in th e refrigerator
- Use the pâton with double turns directly or give on e more simple turn for the pâton with simple turns
