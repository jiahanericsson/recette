  quantity: 10
  unit: g

  quantity: 110
  unit: g

  quantity: 20
  unit: g

  quantity: 60
  unit: g
- name: butter (2)
  quantity: 500
  unit: g
- name: flour (2)
  quantity: 175
  unit: g
steps:
- Mix flour (1), butter (1), salt, sugar
- In a bowl, mix yolks, milk, & water
- Moisten the flour with the bowl mixture and make the dough
- Soften butter (2) and mix with flour (2), then form it in to a square
- Proceed as in traditional puff pastry dough; enclos e the butter  in the dough
