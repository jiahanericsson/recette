---
title: financier
ingredients:
- name: almond / hazelnut / pine nut powder
  quantity: 142
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 265
  unit: g
- name: flour T45
  quantity: 90
  unit: g
- name: egg white
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: browned butter
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
steps:
- let egg white & browned butter come to room temperature
- sift together powder, icing sugar, and flour
- mix in egg white and butter
- refrigerate until cool
- fill as desired
- bake @ 180 C for approximately 16 minutes depending on size
- '[can flavor with vanilla powder, tea powder, shredded coconut, citrus zest, etc.]'
- '[can fill w/ ganaches, curds, compotes, etc.]'
- makes ~30 pcs
---
