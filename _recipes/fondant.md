---
title: fondant
ingredients:
- name: sugar
  quantity: 500
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: glucose
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
steps:
- bring water and sugar to a boil
- add glucose and cook until 115 C
- pour mixture into kitchenaid and whip with paddle attachment until cool and white
- store filmed in the fridge
- use fondant around 37 C
---
