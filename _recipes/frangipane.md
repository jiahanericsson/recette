---
title: frangipane
ingredients:
- name: almond powder
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: cream
  quantity: 30
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 25
  unit: g
- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 3
  unit: g
- name: Pastry Cream
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
steps:
- Cream the butter, sugar and the almond powder
- Add gradually the eggs, liquid cream, and vanilla extract; whip well with whisk
- Finish with adding the flour
- Mix all with the pastry cream
- Whip the cream to provide volume
- (flour is optional)
- (can add a dash of liqueur or flavorings such as earl grey tea)
---
