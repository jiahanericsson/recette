---
title: gaufres (waffles)
ingredients:
- name: Milk
  quantity: 550
  unit: g
- name: vanilla pods
  quantity: 2
  unit: pc
- name: browned butter
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 530
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 6
  unit: g
- name: egg white
  quantity: 220
  unit: g
- name: caster sugar (optional)
  quantity: 40
  unit: g
steps:
- boil milk w/ scraped vanilla, infuse overnight
- sift flour and salt, set aside
- heat infused milk and browned butter until 50 C (add additional milk or butter to achieve total weight)
- pour over dry ingredients in 3 batches to achieve an elastic dough
- whip egg white at room temperature and immediately incorporate into dough
- shake caster sugar into mold when making waffles or immediately after
- freeze immediately if not consuming right away.  reheat in oven for a few minutes.
- can replace a vanilla bean with 2 tbsp of vanilla extract
---
