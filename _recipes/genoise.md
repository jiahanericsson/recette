---
title: genoise
ingredients:
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
steps:
- Mix the eggs and the sugar
- Whisk in the mixer for 20 minutes on medium speed
- Delicately fold in the sifted flour with a flexible  spatula or a skimmer
- Bake in a 20cm diameter ring minimum height 4.5cm at  180 C / 190 C
- Cool completely before removing from the ring
- Make this genoise a day before using it
---
