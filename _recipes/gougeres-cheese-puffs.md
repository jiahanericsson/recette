---
title: gougères (cheese puffs)
ingredients:
- name: Milk
  quantity: 480
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 480
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 320
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 16
  unit: g
- name: pepper &/ chili powder
  quantity: 4
  unit: pinch
- name: Flour
  quantity: 560
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 800
  unit: g
- name: cheese (gruyere, cheddar, parmesan, comté, mimolette, swiss)
  quantity: 440
  unit: g
steps:
- grate the cheese
- heat water, milk, butter, and spices to a boil
- and sifted flour and stir until dough pulls away from side of the pot
- transfer to a mixer with paddle attachment and mix a bit slowly to let heat escape
- when it's not too hot, incorporate eggs slowly
- incorporate all of the cheese
- pipe, freeze to keep
- start at 425 F for 5 minutes, finish at 375 F for 20-25 minutes
- for extra crispy puffs, poke the side of the puff to release steam 5 minutes before finishing baking
- (can optionally incorporate minced fresh herbs like chive, thyme, parsley when adding cheese to dough)
- makes 100 small to mediums pieces
