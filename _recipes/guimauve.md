---
title: guimauve
ingredients:
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: glucose
  quantity: 15
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 30
  unit: g
- name: egg white
  quantity: 35
  unit: g
- name: sugar 2
  quantity: 5
  unit: g
- name: gelatin
  quantity: 5
  unit: g
- name: Vanilla Pod
  quantity: 1
  unit: pc
steps:
- soak gelatin in water
- make syrup by cooking sugar, glucose, and water to 130 C
- in the meanwhile, whip the egg white w/ sugar 2
- pour cooked syrup into egg white
- add gelatin and whip until fluffy and forms stiff peaks
- add scraped vanilla beans
- pipe onto silpat or greased baking trays
- refrigerate for 3 hours or let stand overnight until completely cool
- cut with damp knife or cutter if necessary, dust with granulated sugar, powdered sugar, or corn starch
---
