---
title: joconde
ingredients:
- name: egg
  quantity: 500
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 375
  unit: g
- name: almond powder
  quantity: 375
  unit: g
- name: egg white
  quantity: 350
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 75
  unit: g
steps:
- Cream well the eggs, almond powder and icing sugar
- Whip the egg white with the sugar
- Fold in the egg mixture in the whipped egg whites
- Add the sifted flour and the warm melted butter
- Spread out on baking paper or silpat, 600g each
- Bake for a few minutes at 230/240°C, vent closed
- '(for chocolate joconde: substitute 50g of the flour with cocoa powder)'
- '(for pistachio joconde: add 100g of pistachio paste)'
- '(for decoration dough: cream together equal parts butter, icing sugar, egg white, and flour [or 7:3 ratio of flour and cocoa powder], streak and freeze before pouring joconde batter)'
---
