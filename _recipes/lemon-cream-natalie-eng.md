---
title: lemon cream (Natalie Eng)
ingredients:
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: lemon
  quantity: 1
  unit: pc
- name: lemon juice
  quantity: 80
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: gelatine
  quantity: 1
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 150
  unit: g
steps:
- bloom gelatine in cold water for at least 20 minutes
- zest 1 lemon, heat over bain marie w/ sugar until sugar crystals are fragrant and yellow
- add lemon juice followed by eggs, whisk until mixture thickens
- add gelatine and whisk well to dissolve
- blend in butter with immersion blender without incorporating too much air until smooth and creamy
- let set in fridge, best overnight.
---
