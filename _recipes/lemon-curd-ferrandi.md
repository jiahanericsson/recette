---
title: lemon curd (Ferrandi)
ingredients:
- name: lemon juice
  quantity: 210
  unit: g
- name: lemon zest
  quantity: 3.5
  unit: g
- name: sugar
  quantity: 315
  unit: g
- name: custard powder
  quantity: 18
  unit: g
- name: gelatin
  quantity: 3
  unit: g
- name: eggs
  quantity: 177
  unit: g
- name: yolk
  quantity: 66
  unit: g
- name: butter
  quantity: 180
  unit: g
steps:
- mix sifted custard powder with sugar
- mix in juice, eggs, and yolk
- whisk over a bain marie until thickened and creamy
- add bloomed gelatin and zest, cook until creamier
- take off heat and let cool
- when cooled, add butter at room temperature and blend well
---
