---
title: macaron shell à l’ancienne (michalak)
ingredients:
- name: powdered sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: almonds (no skin)
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: hazelnuts (no skin)
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: egg white
  quantity: 80
  unit: g
- name: caster sugar
  quantity: 25
  unit: g
steps:
- '  make meringue with egg white and caster sugar until medium peaks'
- '  make t.p.t. with powdered sugar and nuts'
- '  mix t.p.t. into meringue'
- '  pipe'
- '  dust with powdered sugar and leave to develop for just a few minutes'
- bake at 428 F for 13 or 14 minutes
- '  [bake with vents closed, first half in the middle and second half towards the bottom of the oven]'
