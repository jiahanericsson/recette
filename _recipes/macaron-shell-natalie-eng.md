---
title: macaron shell (Natalie Eng)
ingredients:
- name: almond powder
  quantity: 300
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 300
  unit: g
- name: caster sugar
  quantity: 300
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 75
  unit: g
- name: egg white (1)
  quantity: 110
  unit: g
- name: egg white (2)
  quantity: 110
  unit: g
steps:
- make t.p.t. w/ almond powder and icing sugar
- cook caster sugar and water until 118 C
- take syrup off the heat, let bubbles disappear, pour over lightly whipped egg white (1) slowly and whisk until cool
- mix egg white (2) with dry ingredients and mix meringue in 3 parts
- pipe and set aside for 30 minutes
- bake at 160 C for 12-13 minutes
---
