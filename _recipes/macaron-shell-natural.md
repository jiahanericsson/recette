---
title: macaron shell (natural)
ingredients:
- name: almond powder
  quantity: 275
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 215
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 275
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 63
  unit: g
- name: egg white (1)
  quantity: 120
  unit: g
- name: egg white (2)
  quantity: 75
  unit: g
steps:
- make t.p.t. w/ almond powder & icing sugar
- make syrup by cooking sugar & water to 117C
- while syrup is cooking, start mounting egg white (1) slowly
- pour syrup into egg white (1) when ready & whip meringue till stiff peaks
- add meringue to egg white (2) & mix well
- sift t.p.t. over meringue mixture & mix well
- 'pipe shells with #6 tip'
- wait until top of shells crust over
- bake shells @ 320-330F for ~10 min, vent open
- take out of oven & leave on rack to cool
---
