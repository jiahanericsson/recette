---
title: madeleine (michalak)
ingredients:
- name: Milk
  quantity: 95
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: vanilla extract (or 1 pod)
  quantity: 13
  unit: g
- name: citrus zest
  quantity: 10
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 315
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 345
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: baking powder
  quantity: 12.5
  unit: g
- name: honey
  quantity: 150
  unit: g
- name: powdered sugar
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: lemon juice
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: olive oil
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
steps:
- mix together eggs, zest, milk, vanilla, and sugar without creating volumn
- add softened butter and sifted flour and baking powder
- rest or freeze
- bake at 392 F
- once out of the oven, immediately pipe honey to the center of each madeleine
- to make glaze, mix powdered sugar, lemon juice, and olive oil without emulsifying
- dip madeleines into glace 5 minutes after baking
- '[makes ~20 madeleines]'
