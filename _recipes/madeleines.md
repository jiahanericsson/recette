  quantity: 15

  quantity: 360

  quantity: 200
  unit: g
steps:
- '  butter and flour the madeleine molds, freeze'
- '  whip eggs, sugar, and salt until frothy and thick'
- sift flour and baking powder over
- '  combine lemon zest and melted butter, and slowly drizzle over batter until just incorporated'
- '  chill batter for at least 1 hour'
- '  fill each mold 3/4 full, can freeze in trays as well'
- bake at 425 F for 8 - 9 minutes
- '  remove from oven and immediately transfer onto cooling rack'
- '  store uncovered for 1 - 3 days'
- '  (can add 5g of tea powder for flavoring)'
