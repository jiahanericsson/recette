---
title: meringue francaise (french meringue)
ingredients:
- name: egg white
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
steps:
- Whip the egg whites; add the sugar in 3 times
- Mix delicately the sifted icing sugar by hand with  a pastry  scrapper or a skimmer
- Pipe into shapes and bake at 100 C maximum or incorporate into pastries
---
