---
title: meringue italienne (italian meringue)
ingredients:
- name: egg white
  quantity: 500
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 1000
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
steps:
- Cook sugar to 121 C
- Start to whip the egg whites when the sugar is at  110 C / 112 C
- '    Pout the cooked sugar gradually over the whipped eg g  whites'
- '    Whip till completely cool'
- Pipe into shapes and bake at 100 C maximum or incorporate into pastries
