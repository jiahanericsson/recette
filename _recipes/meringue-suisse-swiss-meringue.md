---
title: meringue suisse (swiss meringue)
ingredients:
- name: egg white
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
steps:
- Whip egg whites and sugar, warm in bain - marie up to 45 C
- Whip till cool
- Pipe into shapes and bake at 100 C maximum or incorporate into pastries
---
