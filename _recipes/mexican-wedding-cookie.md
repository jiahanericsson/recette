---
title: mexican wedding cookie
ingredients:
- name: butter
  quantity: 454
  unit: g
- name: sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 10
  unit: g
- name: flour
  quantity: 650
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 4
  unit: g
- name: chopped nuts
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
steps:
- cream together butter and powdered sugar
- incorporate vanilla extract
- add in sifted flour and salt
- mix in chopped nuts
- chill the batter, then roll into small balls
- bake at 400 F for 10-12 minutes or until the top and bottom start to get golden brown
- take out of oven and cool until warm before rolling them in powdered sugar with double coating
- sift to remove excess powdered sugar and keep at room temperature
---
