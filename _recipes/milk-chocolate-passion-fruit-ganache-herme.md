---
title: milk chocolate passion fruit ganache (hermé)
ingredients:
- name: passion fruit juice
  quantity: 185
  unit: g
- name: milk chocolate 40%
  quantity: 350
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 70
  unit: g
steps:
- heat juice and chocolate
- at 40 C, add softened butter in small pieces
- mix well and chill until use
