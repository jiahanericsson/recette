---
title: orange ganache montée (Cedric Grolet)
ingredients:
- name: gelatin
  quantity: 4
  unit: g
- name: cream
  quantity: 530
  unit: g
- name: white chocolate
  quantity: 144
  unit: g
- name: orange juice
  quantity: 120
  unit: g
steps:
- prepare the cream mixture 1 day ahead of time
- boil half of the cream and add the bloomed gelatin
- pour the cream mixture bit by bit over the chocolate to emulsify
- add the other half of the cream, then the orange juice
- mix well w/ emersion blender, contact film, and refridgerate for 12 hours before whipping
---
