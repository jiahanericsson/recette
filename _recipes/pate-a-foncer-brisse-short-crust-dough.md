---
title: pate a foncer / brisse (short crust dough)
ingredients:
- name: Flour
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 5
  unit: g
- name: granulated sugar
  quantity: 25
  unit: g
- name: Yolk
  quantity: 1
  unit: pc
- name: water
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
steps:
- dissolve the sugar and salt in water
- '''sabler'' the butter and the flour'
- add the yolk and water, control the consistency of the dough w/ water
- mix the ingredients w/out overworking the dough
- '''fraser'' quickly w/out giving elasticity to the dough'
- store in fridge before use
- bake at 200 C to 210 C
- '[no sugar or yolk for quiche]'
---
