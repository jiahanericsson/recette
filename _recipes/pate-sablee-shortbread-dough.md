---
title: pate sablee (shortbread dough)
ingredients:
- name: Flour
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: baking powder
  quantity: 5
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: egg
  quantity: 1
  unit: pc
- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 1
  unit: dash
steps:
- dissolve the sugar and salt in water
- '''sabler'' the butter and the flour'
- add the egg and water, control the consistency of the dough w/ water
- mix the ingredients w/out overworking the dough
- '''fraser'' quickly w/out giving elasticity to the dough'
- store in fridge before use
- bake at 180 C
