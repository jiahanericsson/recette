---
title: pate sucree (sweet dough)
ingredients:
- name: Flour
  quantity: 250
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: egg
  quantity: 50
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 20
  unit: g
steps:
- make a well w/ sifted flour and baking powder
- cream the butter and icing sugar inside the well
- add the egg
- heat if necessary to achieve buttercream consistency
- mix everything together and adjust the consistency by adding water if necessary
- stop when just mixed
- '''fraser'' w/ a pastry scraper'
- store in fridge before use
- bake at 180 C
- '[if making flavored dough, substitute 25g of flour for 25g of cocoa powder or 10g of flour w/ 10g of tea powder]'
---
