---
title: royal icing (SweetAmbs)
ingredients:
- name: powdered sugar
  quantity: 910
  unit: g
- name: meringue powder
  quantity: 104
  unit: g
- name: water
  quantity: 177
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 1
  unit: g
- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 10
  unit: g
steps:
- beat confection's sugar, meringue powder, and salt on low spedd with paddle attachment until combined
- on low speed, add 1/3 of water at a time; might not need the full portion
- add vanilla extract and mix until combined
- scrape the bowl, continue mixing on low until stiff peak (3-5 min), don't overmix!!!
- use stiff consistency, or add more water for medium or flood consistency
- can keep in fridge film-contact up to 10 days.  mix well by hand when ready to use
---
