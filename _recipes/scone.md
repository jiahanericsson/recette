---
title: scone
ingredients:
- name: Flour
  quantity: 150
  unit: g
- name: pastry flour
  quantity: 150
  unit: g
- name: baking powder
  quantity: 15
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 68
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 68
  unit: g
- name: honey
  quantity: 15
  unit: g
- name: Milk
  quantity: 110
  unit: g
steps:
- mix all ingredients except milk w/ hook attachment
- add milk slowly
- mix everything for 3 minutes on slow and 2 minutes on fast
- roll out into thick slab
- refridgerate until firm
- cut out cylinders with circle cutter (~70g each) and freeze
- when ready to bake, let it come to room temperature andbrush with eggwash
- bake at 180 C for 10 minutes
- take out and keep covered at room temperature
- '[optionally add cubed granny smith apples]'
---
