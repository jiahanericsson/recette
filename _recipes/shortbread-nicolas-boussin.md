---
title: shortbread (Nicolas Boussin)
ingredients:
- name: butter
  quantity: 80
  unit: g
- name: flour
  quantity: 90
  unit: g
- name: icing sugar
  quantity: 40
  unit: g
- name: orange
  quantity: 0.5
  unit: pc
- name: salt
  quantity: 1
  unit: g
- name: vanilla bean
  quantity: 0.5
  unit: pc
steps:
- scrape the vanilla bean seeds and combine with softened butter, icing sugar, orange zest, and salt
- sift the flour over, incorporate and chill
- roll out to 4 mm thickness and cut int 4.5 cm circles
- bake at 150 C in a convection oven for 10 minutes
---
