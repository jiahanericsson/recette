---
title: speculos
ingredients:
- name: Butter
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: brown sugar
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 60
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 40
  unit: g
- name: Milk
  quantity: 16
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 400
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 10
  unit: g
- name: cinnamon powder
  quantity: 2
  unit: g
- name: baking powder
  quantity: 10
  unit: g
steps:
- cream together butter, brown sugar, sugar, salt, and cinnamon powder
- add eggs one by one
- add milk
- finish with sifted flour and baking powder
- chill in fridge until firm, roll out and cut into desired shape and size
- bake at 170 C on silpat
---
