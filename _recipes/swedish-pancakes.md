---
title: Swedish Pancakes
ingredients:
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Milk
  quantity: 500
  unit: g
- name: Flour
  quantity: 70
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 15
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 1
  unit: g
- name: Butter
  quantity: 30
  unit: g
steps:
- melt the butter
- in a tall vessel, blend the eggs and the milk
- add flour, sugar, salt, and melted butter; blend well
- cook on medium-low heat
---
