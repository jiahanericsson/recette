---
title: tempered chocolate (seeding)
ingredients:
- name: chocolate (1)
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: tempered chocolate pieces (2)
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
steps:
- 'melt chocolate (1) over a bain marie until temperature reaches: 55 C - 58 C for bittersweet and dark, or 45 C - 50 C for milk and white chocolate'
- remove from bain marie and add chocolate (2)
- 'stir vigorously and constantly until temperature reaches: 28 C - 29 C for bittersweet and dark, or 27 C - 28 C for milk chocolate, or 26 - 27 C for white chocolate'
- 'return mixture back to the bain marie until temperature reaches: 31 C - 32 C for bittersweet and dark chocolate, 29 C - 30 C for milk chocolate, and 28 C - 29 C for white chocolate'
- use chocolate, maintain desired temperature range
---
