---
title: Torta Caprese (Emiko Davies)
ingredients:
- name: almond meal
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: 70% chocolate
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: butter
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: sugar
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: eggs
  quantity: 3
  unit: pc
steps:
- grease and line a 20 cm or 8 inch round cake tin with baking paper
- preheat oven to 320 F
- separate the egg white and yolk, let it come to room temperature
- melt chocolate over bain marie
- remove from heat and add in butter until melted, then add the sugar and almond meal
- once mixture is no longer hot, add in the egg yolk
- whip egg white into soft peaks and fold into the mixture
- pour batter into cake tin and bake for ~40 minutes
- keep in fridge for several days and dust with powdered sugar before serving
- (makes 1 8 inch cake)
---
