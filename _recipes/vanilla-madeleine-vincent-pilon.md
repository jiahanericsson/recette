---
title: vanilla madeleine (vincent pilon)
ingredients:
- name: Butter
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: Sugar
  quantity: 240
  unit: g
- name: Eggs
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: vanilla beans
  quantity: 2
  unit: pc
- name: Flour
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: baking powder
  quantity: 8
  unit: g
steps:
- combine softened butter with sugar
- add eggs, then flour sifted with baking powder
- rest the batter a minimum of 2 hours
- bake at 390 F for approximately 12mn (vent open)
---
