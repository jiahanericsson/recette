---
title: vanilla sablés (Dorie Greenspan)
ingredients:
- name: butter
  quantity: 226
  unit: g
- name: sugar
  quantity: 100
  unit: g
- name: powdered sugar
  quantity: 30
  unit: g
- name: salt
  quantity: 2
  unit: g
- name: yolk
  quantity: 40
  unit: g
- name: vanilla extract
  quantity: 2
  unit: g
- name: flour
  quantity: 272
  unit: g
steps:
- beat butter, both sugars, and salt on medium with paddle attachment for 3 minutes until smooth but not fluffy
- beat in yolks and vanilla on low
- add flour all at once, pulse, then mix on low, until flour disappears
- rest, roll out until 1/4 inch thick, cut and refrigerate or freeze
- bake for 16 to 19 minutes at 350 F
- makes about 30 2-inch cut cookies
---
