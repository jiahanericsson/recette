---
title: whisky truffle
ingredients:
- name: dark chocolate 55%
  quantity: 150
  unit: g
- name: milk chocolate
  quantity: 125
  unit: g
- name: cream
  quantity: 200
  unit: g
- name: whisky (optional)
  quantity: 30
  unit: g
steps:
- pour boiled cream onto chocolate and let stand for a few minutes
- add whisky if using, mix with a spatula to emulsify
- leave to firm up and roll into balls by hand
- double dip in dark chocolate
- roll in cocoa powder immediately after the second coating
- sieve to remove excess cocoa powder
- conserve at 16 C
---
