---
title: yuzu chocolate ganache (michalak)
ingredients:
- name: heavy cream
  quantity: 160
  unit: g
- name: milk chocolate
  quantity: 120
  unit: g
- name: gelatin
  quantity: 2
  unit: g
- name: yuzu juice
  quantity: 30
  unit: g
steps:
- pour saturated gelatin, juice, and hot cream over chocolate and blend to make an emulsion
- contact film and chill in the fridge
- whisk to achieve desired texture before using
- (if using concentrated puree, substitute 30g juice with 15g of concentrate and 15g of cream)
---
